Sources

Where the data, models and software came from

Data

  • BCI Competition IV, dataset 2a: 9 people, 4 imagined movements, 22 EEG channels. Brunner, Leeb, Müller-Putz, Schlögl and Pfurtscheller, Graz University of Technology, 2008.
  • BCI Competition IV, dataset 2b: 9 people, left vs right hand, 3 channels. Leeb, Brunner, Müller-Putz, Schlögl and Pfurtscheller, 2008.
  • PhysioNet EEG Motor Movement/Imagery Dataset: 109 people, 64 channels; this app uses the first 10. Schalk et al., IEEE Trans. Biomed. Eng. 51(6), 2004, shared through PhysioNet (Goldberger et al., Circulation 101(23), 2000).

Models

  • EEGNet: Lawhern et al., “EEGNet: a compact convolutional neural network for EEG-based brain–computer interfaces”, J. Neural Eng. 15(5), 2018.
  • FBCSP: Ang, Chin, Zhang and Guan, “Filter Bank Common Spatial Pattern (FBCSP) in brain-computer interface”, IJCNN 2008.
  • CSP + LDA: Ramoser, Müller-Gerking and Pfurtscheller, “Optimal spatial filtering of single trial EEG during imagined hand movement”, IEEE Trans. Rehab. Eng. 8(4), 2000.

Software

  • MOABB downloads and epochs the datasets (Jayaram and Barachant, J. Neural Eng. 15(6), 2018).
  • MNE-Python for filtering and CSP, scikit-learn for LDA and logistic regression, and PyTorch for EEGNet.
